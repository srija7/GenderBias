# GenderBias
Unveiling Gender Bias: An Eye-Tracking Analysis of Scene Perception

Persistent Gender Bias: Despite efforts to address gender disparities, biases continue to influence attitudes and actions, subtly impacting scene perception and decision-making.
Complex Cognitive Processes: The relationship between gender bias and cognitive processing, as indicated by physiological responses such as pupil dilation, remains underexplored.
Technological Integration: Utilizing cutting-edge eye-tracking technology to monitor visual attention and cognitive load can provide deeper insights into how gender bias affects scene perception.
Experimental Investigation: Analyzing participant responses to gender-related scene modifications through controlled experiments can uncover underlying biases and inform strategies to mitigate their impact
